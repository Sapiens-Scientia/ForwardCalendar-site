Forward Calendar

Privacy policy

Updated October 13, 2026

Forward Calendar is a personal calendar app for Mac and iPhone. This policy describes the direct Google Calendar integration and local storage in the app.

Google account and calendar access

Google Sign-In authenticates your account. Forward uses basic account identity, including your account identifier and email address, to associate the correct local cache with your account and display connection status.

Forward requests permission to read your calendar list and to view and manage Google Calendar events. It uses these permissions to display accessible calendars and events, and to create, update, move, or delete events when you request a change and your account has permission. Event data may include titles, dates, times, time zones, descriptions, locations, recurrence, reminders, and other event metadata. Unedited metadata is preserved.

Requests go from your device directly to Google using Google’s Sign-In SDK and Calendar API. Forward does not operate a calendar backend. Google’s handling of authentication and API requests is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Local storage

Forward stores calendar information, fetched events, range freshness, and unfinished drafts in an account-scoped cache on your device. Cached events support offline reading. Drafts remain local until you explicitly save them to Google. The Google Sign-In SDK manages credentials using Apple Keychain; Forward does not store tokens in its preferences or source configuration.

App preferences include appearance, magnification, pane, week start, calendar selection, and editing lock. Forward does not access Apple Calendar or Apple Reminders.

Use and sharing

Forward uses Google data to provide the calendar features you request. It has no advertising or Forward analytics service. It does not sell calendar data or send it to a Forward server. Your requested event changes are sent to Google; Google Calendar’s existing sharing and invitation rules continue to apply.

Forward Calendar’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deletion and control

Signing out removes that account’s Forward cache and drafts from the device and clears its sign-in credentials. It does not delete your Google Calendar records. You can also remove Forward’s access through your Google account’s third-party connections settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local app storage according to the operating system’s behavior.

This website

This informational site uses no analytics, cookies, or third-party scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.
