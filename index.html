Forward Calendar

A clear view of your time, connected directly to Google Calendar.

Forward presents your calendar as a continuous timeline. Future events appear above the present, and an event’s height represents its duration. Four magnifications let you move between the day and the larger picture.

Your calendar, directly

Connect one Google account on each device to view its accessible calendars, create events, and edit or delete events where your account has permission. Google Calendar remains the source of record. Forward does not import your Apple Calendar or Apple Reminders records.

Available offline

Previously loaded events remain readable on your device. Unfinished edits are saved as local drafts. Committing changes to Google requires an internet connection.

Simple and readable

Neutral surfaces, clear typography, and calendar-color markers keep the calendar usable in light and dark appearances.

Forward is currently intended for personal use. There is no public download or hosted calendar service.
